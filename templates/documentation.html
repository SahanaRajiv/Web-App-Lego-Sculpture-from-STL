My LEGO Project!

I built software that lets you take any 3D design you find online or create yourself, and it shows you how to build it with real LEGO bricks. My online tool:

  • Turns digital 3D models into LEGO creations.
  • Gives easy-to-follow, step-by-step pictures like a LEGO manual.
  • Tells you exactly which color bricks you need.
  • Makes sure a creation will not have unsupported or floating pieces.
  • Creates a new 3D model of the LEGO creation or printable PDF instructions.

It is fun, but it also helps you understand STEM: how 3D shapes work, how digital ideas become physical objects, and some of the smart ways engineers build things.

Coding Issues and How I Solved Them!

01

The Challenge

When I wanted to show my app online, my browser gave me an ERR_SSL_PROTOCOL_ERROR.

02

Decoding the Problem

The app was talking HTTP while the browser expected HTTPS. The browser remembered past secure chats and would not allow the less-secure connection.

03

The Fix

I used a certificate and configured a reverse proxy to handle the secure conversations. The browser could then connect safely.

Cursor helped me understand HTTP versus HTTPS, browser security, and how to make the app accessible to everyone.
