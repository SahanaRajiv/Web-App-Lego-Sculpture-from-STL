*A Stereolithography (STL) file is a standard file format used for 3D printing and computer-aided design (CAD). It represents a 3D model as a series of connected triangles, describing only the surface geometry of a three-dimensional object without any color, texture, or other common CAD model attributes.

Build & Donate a LEGO Duck Kit

Make a difference by building a LEGO duck kit and donating it to a child in need! Follow the instructions to assemble your own kit, then donate it to Second Chance Toys to bring joy and creativity to children.

Bricks Needed for the Kit:

  • 1x1 bricks: 1 (pink/red color)
  • 1x2 bricks: 5 (olive/gray color)
  • 1x3 bricks: 5 (green color)
  • 1x4 bricks: 6 (dark purple color)
  • 1x6 bricks: 34 (purple color)
  • 2x4 bricks: 5 (teal color)
